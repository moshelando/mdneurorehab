What changed
Richer homepage, clearer visitor pathways, explicit review labels, stronger placeholders, content-status tracking, no-index safeguards, and a dedicated decision guide.

How to review the prototype

Review the site by category rather than trying to approve every sentence immediately. First approve the basic model and sitemap. Then confirm the actual services and programs. Only after that should the final slogans and page copy be written.

First

Approve the structure

Are programs, disciplines, conditions, techniques, and goals separated correctly? Is the navigation understandable?

Second

Confirm what is true

Which programs, clinicians, techniques, payers, ages, and referral pathways actually exist?

Third

Approve the message

What should the practice be known for? Which claims and differentiators can be supported?

Fourth

Fill the placeholders

Supply photos, biographies, hours, phone, insurance details, forms, legal language, and other final information.

Identity and messaging

The prototype positions the practice as a multidisciplinary rehabilitation center whose clearest specialty is neurological rehabilitation.

Review Mission & Messaging Page

Programs and services

The prototype separates clinical disciplines from coordinated programs. This is the most important structural correction to the current site.

Condition library

The prototype uses a central searchable directory plus a limited number of standalone pages. The final selection should be informed by actual clinical priorities and search data.

Review Conditions Directory

Functionality and forms

The current forms are demonstrations only. The production system must be chosen after deciding what information should be collected and whether any workflow may involve protected health information.

Visual direction

The current direction is calm, clinical, warm, and modern without resembling a hospital system. It uses navy and teal for trust and clarity, coral for action, generous spacing, and restrained illustrative graphics.

Required before production launch

Practice information

Phone, hours, address details, parking, accessibility, insurance, referral requirements, and response times.

Clinical verification

Programs, disciplines, techniques, condition descriptions, staff credentials, age ranges, and scope of practice.

Brand assets

Final logo decision, photography, staff portraits, facility images, testimonials, mission, and slogan.

Technical decisions

WordPress setup, hosting, forms, scheduling, security, analytics, backups, integrations, and maintenance.

Professional review

Privacy, terms, accessibility, medical disclaimer, form handling, and any legal or healthcare-compliance questions.

Migration

Search Console audit, URL map, redirects, metadata, structured data, testing, and post-launch verification.
