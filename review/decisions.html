Prototype 3.0 Decision List

Use this as the meeting agenda.
Answer structural and factual questions first. Final wording and visual polish come afterward.

1. Identity, audience, and message

Should the site lead with multidisciplinary neurological rehabilitation, broad outpatient rehabilitation, pediatric care, or another emphasis?

Shown in: Homepage, Mission, About

Rank adult patients, caregivers, pediatric families, physicians, case managers, attorneys, schools, and other referral sources.

Shown in: Homepage and navigation

Choose three to five supportable areas of expertise or differentiators.

Shown in: Homepage and program hierarchy

Confirm Maryland Neuro-Rehab & Wellness Center, abbreviations, pediatric sub-branding, and logo use.

Shown in: Header, footer, metadata

Select or revise the working options only after the identity and evidence are confirmed.

Shown in: Mission and homepage

2. Sitemap and visitor pathways

Home, Programs, Therapy Services, Conditions, Patients & Referrals, About, and Contact.

Shown in: Global navigation

Programs organize coordinated care; therapy services identify professional disciplines.

Shown in: Programs and Services hubs

Approve, combine, postpone, or remove each proposed page.

Shown in: Sitemap

Define the main actions for patients, families, new patients, and referring providers.

Shown in: Homepage and calls to action

3. Rehabilitation programs

Confirm or replace Neurological Rehabilitation; Brain Injury & Stroke; Balance, Gait & Fall Prevention; Pediatric; and Pain, Orthopedic & Post-Surgical.

Shown in: Programs hub

Rank the program areas by strategic importance and actual capability.

Shown in: Homepage

Confirm disciplines, eligibility, age ranges, leadership, visit structure, caregiver involvement, and referral rules.

Shown in: Program detail pages

Confirm whether “Hannah’s Bananas” remains public and how it relates to the main practice.

Shown in: Pediatric program

4. Clinical disciplines

Confirm Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Speech-Language Therapy, and any additional professional disciplines.

Shown in: Therapy Services

Specify ages, clinicians, locations, and any limitations.

Shown in: Service detail pages

Supply clinician names, licenses, certifications, and areas of expertise.

Shown in: Team and service pages

Describe the actual communication, evaluation, planning, and handoff process.

Shown in: Homepage and program pages

5. Treatments, techniques, and equipment

Review every item in the techniques directory and remove unsupported methods.

Shown in: Treatments & Techniques

Confirm Kinesio Taping, electrical stimulation, vestibular treatment, feeding, swallowing, cognitive rehabilitation, and device training.

Shown in: Technique descriptions

Use clinical importance, patient usefulness, and search value—not mere availability.

Shown in: SEO and content plan

Identify only items that are current, relevant, and supportable.

Shown in: Programs, services, facility

6. Conditions and medical content

Confirm the full directory against actual clinicians, programs, age ranges, and referral patterns.

Shown in: Conditions directory

Choose based on central expertise, usefulness, and verified search traffic.

Shown in: Condition pages

Retain and rewrite valuable pages; consolidate or redirect overlapping and low-value pages.

Shown in: Migration plan

Assign qualified reviewers for conditions, programs, techniques, and claims.

Shown in: All clinical content

7. Evidence, team, and trust

Use credentials, processes, equipment, access, outcomes, or approved patient experience—not generic superlatives.

Shown in: Homepage

Supply roster, roles, credentials, specialties, biographies, photographs, and acceptance status.

Shown in: Team page

Confirm location, hours, parking, accessibility, transportation, and photographs.

Shown in: Facility and Contact

Confirm permission, exact wording, attribution, and any required disclaimers.

Shown in: Patient Stories

8. Forms, scheduling, and referrals

Choose phone-first, appointment request, direct scheduling, external portal, or a combination.

Shown in: Request Appointment

Minimize sensitive data and determine whether any workflow involves protected health information.

Shown in: All forms

Choose secure fax, portal, EHR workflow, or another approved system.

Shown in: Referring Providers

Define routing, confirmations, notifications, response times, retention, and follow-up.

Shown in: Forms and operational copy

9. Visual direction and assets

Assess the calm, warm, modern clinical style, typography, colors, spacing, and illustration approach.

Shown in: Whole prototype

Supply final logo files, brand colors, photography, staff portraits, facility images, and usage rights.

Shown in: Design system

Set the balance before final templates are built.

Shown in: Homepage and templates

10. Production, legal, and launch

Confirm WordPress setup, builder or theme, forms, security, backups, analytics, and maintenance.

Shown in: Technical plan

Final policies must match actual forms, vendors, cookies, analytics, scheduling, and data handling.

Shown in: Legal pages

Audit Search Console and analytics, map old URLs, preserve valuable pages, and implement redirects.

Shown in: SEO migration

Assign leadership, clinical, operational, legal, and technical approval responsibilities.

Shown in: Launch checklist
