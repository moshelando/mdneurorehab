About

Mission, Positioning & Voice

A working messaging framework for discussion—not final approved brand language.

Working copyLeadership approval needed
Purpose of this page: Establish what the practice wants to be known for before writing the final homepage and service copy.

Working positioning statement

Maryland Neuro-Rehab provides coordinated, practical rehabilitation centered on the functions that matter in everyday life: moving, communicating, thinking, caring for oneself, participating in family and community, and adapting to change.

Working mission direction

To help adults and children pursue greater function, independence, and participation through individualized rehabilitation, coordinated clinical disciplines, and respectful partnership with patients, families, caregivers, and referral sources.

Proposed message hierarchy

  1. Primary: Coordinated multidisciplinary rehabilitation focused on real-life function.
  2. Secondary: Neurological rehabilitation as the practice’s clearest distinguishing expertise.
  3. Supporting: Adult and pediatric care, practical goals, family involvement, and an accessible outpatient setting.

Tagline directions

Direction A

Care connected around your life.

Emphasizes coordination and practical participation.

Direction B

Move forward with coordinated care.

Emphasizes progress while remaining restrained.

Direction C

Rehabilitation for the life beyond therapy.

Emphasizes real-world goals rather than isolated exercises.

Voice principles

  • Direct, warm, and clinically restrained
  • Specific about what the practice actually does
  • Focused on function and participation rather than miraculous outcomes
  • Understandable to patients without sounding simplistic to referral sources
  • Clear about uncertainty, evaluation, and individualized treatment
Information needed to finalize the brand message
  • What does the practice believe it does better or differently than nearby alternatives?
  • Which patient populations and referral relationships are most important?
  • Which programs should be the public face of the practice?
  • What parts of the current name and pediatric brand should remain?
  • What claims can be supported by credentials, outcomes, or established processes?

Decision required

Leadership should choose the positioning direction, preferred tone, and one tagline direction before final page copy is approved.
