Cerebral Palsy & Pediatric Neurological Conditions

A family-centered explanation of coordinated pediatric rehabilitation for movement, communication, feeding, sensory, and participation needs.

Pediatric RehabilitationClinical review required

Needs that may bring someone to rehabilitation

  • Motor and mobility needs
  • Communication or feeding difficulty
  • Sensory and regulation needs
  • Self-care and school participation
  • Equipment or positioning needs

Relevant clinical disciplines

  • Pediatric Physical Therapy
  • Pediatric Occupational Therapy
  • Pediatric Speech-Language Therapy

Treatment may include

  • Motor-skill development
  • Adaptive-equipment assessment
  • Communication and feeding therapy
  • Sensory and daily-living support
  • Family coaching

Possible functional goals

  • Support development
  • Increase participation
  • Build practical family strategies
  • Improve access across daily settings
No guaranteed outcomes: Final copy should describe possible rehabilitation goals and individualized care, not promise recovery, cure, or prevention of lasting effects.
