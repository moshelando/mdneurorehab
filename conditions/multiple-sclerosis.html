Multiple Sclerosis & Progressive Neurological Conditions

Progressive neurological conditions may cause changing difficulties with mobility, fatigue, balance, cognition, communication, or daily activity. Rehabilitation can adapt over time.

Physical TherapyOccupational TherapySpeech-Language Therapy

How rehabilitation may relate

This page should focus on the functional difficulties that may bring a person to rehabilitation—not attempt to reproduce a general medical reference article.

The evaluation may consider movement, balance, communication, cognition, self-care, endurance, equipment, home or school demands, caregiver needs, and the person’s own priorities.
