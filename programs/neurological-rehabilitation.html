Neurological Rehabilitation

Multidisciplinary care for people whose movement, balance, communication, cognition, or independence has been affected by a neurological condition.

Physical TherapyOccupational TherapySpeech-Language Therapy
Practice-specific information needed

Confirm before final writing: eligibility, age range, referral requirements, disciplines actually involved, program leadership, visit structure, distinctive equipment or methods, caregiver involvement, and the clearest reasons to choose this program.

Who this program may serve

  • Adults and children living with neurological diagnoses
  • People experiencing changes in movement, coordination, balance, speech, cognition, or daily function
  • Families and caregivers seeking coordinated outpatient support

How care may be organized

The program may draw from several clinical disciplines according to the person’s evaluation, medical needs, functional limitations, and goals.

Physical TherapyOccupational TherapySpeech-Language Therapy

Common treatment elements

  • Gait and balance training
  • Functional mobility training
  • Cognitive-communication therapy
  • Activities-of-daily-living retraining
  • Caregiver education

Functional goals

  • Improve safe movement
  • Strengthen communication and cognition
  • Support everyday independence
  • Increase participation at home and in the community

Related conditions

  • Parkinson’s disease
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Progressive neurological conditions
  • Other neurological diagnoses
Clinical review required: This prototype describes a proposed content structure. The practice must confirm the patients served, services available, staff credentials, and treatment language before publication.
