Principal areas of care

Rehabilitation programs

Programs organize one or more therapy disciplines around a defined group of patient needs. The five shown here are working categories that require practice confirmation.

1

Neurological Rehabilitation

For people whose movement, balance, communication, cognition, or independence has been affected by a neurological condition.

View program model →
2

Brain Injury & Stroke Rehabilitation

Coordinated care addressing mobility, communication, cognition, self-care, and participation after stroke or acquired brain injury.

View program model →
3

Balance, Gait & Fall Prevention

Evaluation and treatment for walking difficulty, dizziness, balance loss, fall risk, and reduced confidence with movement.

View program model →
4

Pediatric Rehabilitation

Physical, occupational, speech, feeding, and developmental support for children and their families.

View program model →
5

Pain, Orthopedic & Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation for pain, injury, surgery recovery, and musculoskeletal limitations affecting movement and daily life.

View program model →
