Occupational Therapy

Care focused on the practical skills needed for everyday life at home, school, work, and in the community.

Neurological RehabilitationBrain Injury & Stroke RehabilitationBalance, Gait & Fall Prevention
Discipline details to confirm

Needed from the practice: current clinicians and credentials, adult and pediatric availability, specialties, certifications, equipment, evaluation process, typical referral reasons, and any treatment methods that should or should not be advertised.

What this discipline may address

  • Dressing, bathing, grooming, and meal preparation
  • Upper-extremity and hand function
  • Cognition, attention, and problem-solving
  • Sensory processing
  • Adaptive equipment and environmental modification
  • Return to school, work, or community roles

Programs in which it may participate

  • Neurological Rehabilitation
  • Brain Injury & Stroke Rehabilitation
  • Balance, Gait & Fall Prevention
  • Pediatric Rehabilitation
  • Pain, Orthopedic & Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Common treatments and techniques

  • Activities-of-daily-living training
  • Cognitive rehabilitation
  • Upper-extremity retraining
  • Hand therapy
  • Sensory-based intervention
  • Splinting and positioning
  • Adaptive-equipment training
  • Home and workplace modification
The treatment plan is individualized.
